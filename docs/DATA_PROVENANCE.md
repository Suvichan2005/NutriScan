# Data provenance and licensing

NutriScan distinguishes package observations, user corrections, deterministic calculations, source metadata, product-database records and research abstracts. A value is not promoted from one category into another without preserving the original provenance.

- **Image observations:** label text is produced in the browser with pinned Tesseract.js and bundled English trained data. Retail barcode reads use the browser's optional native [`BarcodeDetector` API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/BarcodeDetector) when available, accept only checksum-valid EAN/UPC values, and retain source-image IDs. MDN marks this API as limited availability/experimental and secure-context-only; barcode detection is not a verified product identity. Confidence is an OCR score, not factual certainty. Original selected images are not part of repository data.
- **Nutrition calculations:** each parsed nutrient links to its value row and, when scaling relies on a separate header, the observed basis and declared serving-size lines. Identical readings from different photos may be deduplicated numerically while retaining the observations from both images. A calculation is not stronger than these underlying readings.
- **Nutrition source corpus:** small curated set of linked WHO, FAO/Codex, FSSAI and NIDDK pages in `src/modules/knowledge/index.ts`. Each entry records publication scope and limitations in code. WHO trans-fat context is population guidance, not a product-specific amount or personal target. The FSSAI trans-fat source is a dated Gazette instrument about industrial trans fat in products using edible oils/fats, not a consolidated current-law or exact-package compliance determination. The FSSAI Advertising and Claims compendium and amendment index support only a dated, generic explanation of nutrient-content claim wording; redistribution terms were not independently reviewed, and these sources do not validate OCR, determine a package's basis or establish current legal status. Verify later amendments, product scope and current status before a consequential legal use.
- **Additive identity references:** the local corpus includes the Codex GSFA search for lecithins (INS 322) and its distinct 322(i)/322(ii) entries, plus the amorphous silicon dioxide (INS 551) identity record. Stored text is an original brief summary with a source link; downstream reuse terms were not independently reviewed. A generic lecithin label does not establish subtype. Neither record proves ingredient presence, dose, national permission or individual safety.
- **Indian dietary guidance metadata:** the local corpus links to ICMR-NIN's 2024 Dietary Guidelines publication, with bibliographic metadata only. The official publication notice limits electronic product use without prior written permission; guideline content is not reproduced in NutriScan.
- **Indian food-composition research:** the [Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB)](https://www.anuvaad.org.in/indian-nutrient-databank/) offers a downloadable recipe database and describes 1,014 recipes. Its methodology says recipe nutrients primarily use IFCT 2017/2004, supplemented with UK and US tables. The reviewed portal and [INDB repository README](https://github.com/lindsayjaacks/Indian-Nutrient-Databank-INDB-/blob/main/README.md) did not establish redistribution terms for the workbook; the README says IFCT source tables must be requested from ICMR-NIN. The paper's open-access status does not by itself license the workbook or underlying source data. NutriScan does not ingest or redistribute INDB values until the applicable dataset terms are confirmed.
- **Food records:** product lookup is an explicit user action. Exact barcode and candidate enrichment use the current Open Food Facts v3 product endpoint; text search uses Search-a-licious. The official API documentation marks v2 deprecated and v3 current. The database is licensed under ODbL and individual contents under the Database Contents License; product images have a separate CC BY-SA license and are not displayed or copied by this feature. The UI links each record to its source and labels ingredient/allergen fields as community data, separate from package-photo observations. Records may be incomplete, incorrect, stale or refer to another formulation and are not proof of local stock. See the [Open Food Facts API and license guidance](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/) and [license details](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/openfoodfacts-server/api/tutorials/license-be-on-the-legal-side/).
- **Scientific search:** Europe PMC results provide candidate article/abstract metadata. Search results are not automatically quality assessments or consensus.
- **Regulatory document text:** FSSAI Gazette PDFs are fetched only from a fixed allowlist, with byte, page and extracted-character limits; selectable text is an excerpt, not an interpreted rule or legal verdict. Scanned PDF pages are not OCRed except where an entry explicitly permits bounded, labeled machine OCR. `pdfjs-dist` is pinned in the lockfile and licensed Apache-2.0. FSSAI's official additive compendium is a nonbinding source aid; its page says the original Gazette notifications prevail. Its official site copyright policy requires accurate, non-misleading reproduction with source acknowledgement and excludes material identified as third-party. The complete Appendix A PDF is not stored in the repository; exact category conditions and later amendments still require Gazette review.
- **Local text embeddings:** pinned MiniLM model metadata, hash and Apache-2.0 license are recorded in `data/semantic-model-manifest.json`. The downloaded model and local index cache are regenerable and ignored; the small curated index and evaluation records are committed for inspection.
- **Synthetic fixtures:** developer-authored labels and annotations are marked as synthetic and never imply accuracy on real food packs.
- **Public product photographs:** the prior real-photo manifest records source URLs and image hashes. Original photos are kept under ignored `docs/evidence/private/`; do not redistribute unless the source license and attribution terms have been checked.

The current repository does not yet contain a comprehensive machine-readable license ledger for every source image, dataset, and model artifact. Review redistribution rights before external publication.
