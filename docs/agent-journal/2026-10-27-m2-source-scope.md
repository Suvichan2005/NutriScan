## Production answer-path audit — 27 October

Code-path inspection found that the natural Gemini answer path runs source identity/topic/jurisdiction filtering before numeric validation and chooses consumer cards from named sources in the surviving answer. The distinct structured `validateInferenceOutput`/claim-span path is not invoked by the production `generateAgentOutput` flow. Therefore a relevant citation can still be topically valid without semantically supporting the sentence, and an unreferenced non-numeric effect claim previously passed.

The initial implementation trial added an English pattern gate for uncited health effects and advice. On rereading the handoff alongside the 24 October architecture decision, I removed it: this is precisely the kind of phrase classifier that the owner forbids, and it would not establish claim support. The production route still applies its existing numeric and source-identity/topic/jurisdiction checks; non-numeric support remains open. No Gemini/provider request, human reference, held-out case or frozen final experiment was used.
